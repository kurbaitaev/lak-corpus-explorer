How validation works

Independent contributions, documented evidence, consensus, and expert review protect the canonical corpus.

One small task at a time

Answer one focused question independently. Other votes appear only after submission.

Add a correction, evidence note, and source reference when available.

Roles and expertise

Contributor — everyone who registers. Contributors validate, flag, and suggest.

Trusted validator resolves disputed items after expertise is recorded.

Verified expert may issue expert verification.

Administrator manages roles, audits, and appeals.

Expert roles require a documented basis.

Community agreement and expert checks are different

Items move through clear states: pending → community consensus or disputed → expert verified or rejected.

Three independent, reliability-weighted votes can establish community consensus. Disagreement routes the item to specialist review. Consensus is not expert verification.

Points reward quality, not clicks

Points remain provisional until supported by consensus, a reference answer, or expert adjudication.

Rate limits, daily caps, and an audit trail discourage low-quality submissions.

Privacy

Email is private. Profiles and leaderboard listing are opt-in. Votes remain auditable.

Appeals

Submit appeals from your profile. Decisions remain in the permanent audit trail.
