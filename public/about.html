Methodology

General search. Russian aliases expand to attested Lak equivalents; direct text matching remains available.

Normalisation. Search uses Unicode NFKC, е/ё equivalence, and canonical palochka handling.

Structured search. Source annotations support exact wordform, lemma, and grammatical-tag queries.

Source fidelity. Historical and OCR material is preserved verbatim and labelled.

Dialect varieties

  • Standard: literary Lak based on the Kumukh dialect.
  • Arakul: variety associated with Arakul.
  • Balkhar: variety associated with Balkhar.
  • Shali: variety associated with Shali.
  • Historical: forms attested in historical sources.
  • Unspecified: variety absent from source metadata.

Contribute

Choose the task that matches your expertise:

  • Lak speakers: validate translations, spelling, and variety labels.
  • Linguists: review morphology, glosses, and alignments.
  • Archivists and rights specialists: verify provenance and reuse terms.
  • NLP researchers: design reproducible benchmarks and evaluation methods.

Open validation tasks or create an account. Approval rights are role-controlled.

Language information

Lak (лакку маз; ISO 639-3: lbe) is a Northeast Caucasian language of Dagestan.

Modern Lak uses Cyrillic, including palochka (Ӏ).
